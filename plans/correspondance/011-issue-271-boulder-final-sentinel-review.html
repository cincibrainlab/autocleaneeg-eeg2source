Senior Review Result

No actionable finding remains in the narrow sentinel revision. Boulder 009's P1 is resolved. ParallelProcessor.process_batch maps None and -1 to the constructor's already resolved positive self.n_jobs, rejects booleans, non-integers, zero, and other negative values, and passes only a positive integer to ProcessPoolExecutor. The CLI independently maps an explicit user -1 to processor.n_jobs, preventing the former executor error and individual-file fallback. CLI help now states the sentinel behavior.

The correction does not widen nested source concurrency. Sequential and Parallel forward work remains n_jobs=1; the inspected GPU CuPy, PyTorch, and TensorFlow inverse fallbacks remain n_jobs=1; MNE 1.6 Raw inverse and Parallel Epochs paths do not receive the resolved outer worker count. The module-level process worker continues to construct its per-file processor with n_jobs=1. Explicit -1 therefore retains legacy outer/label-processing semantics without leaking all-core behavior into source/inverse work.
